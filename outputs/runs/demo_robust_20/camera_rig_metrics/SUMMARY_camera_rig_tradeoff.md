# 3Cam To 4CamAsym Trade-Off

This is the main head-to-head stakeholder comparison: one extra camera and additional depth data are exchanged for more redundant observability and less single-view/blind surface.

|Metric|3Cam|4CamAsym|Change|Unit|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Redundant Surface Gain|44.0|65.4|21.4|percentage points|
|Strong Redundancy Gain|7.3|28.9|21.6|percentage points|
|Single-View Surface Reduction|39.3|23.6|15.7|percentage points|
|Blind Surface Reduction|15.8|10.1|5.6|percentage points|
|Not-Proven-Free Volume Reduction|9.9|8.3|1.5|percentage points|
|Added Raw Depth Data|3.7|4.9|1.2|MB/sample|
|Added Cameras|3.0|4.0|1.0|camera|
